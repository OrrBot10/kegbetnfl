    How it works

    Each owner drafted 3 NFL teams. Only regular-season wins count. Ties count as neither a win nor a loss.

    The owner with the fewest combined wins after Week 18 buys a keg for everyone else. If owners tie for last, they split it.

    Scores come straight from ESPN every time this page loads, so the standings are always current. To see another season, add ?season=2027 to the end of the address.
